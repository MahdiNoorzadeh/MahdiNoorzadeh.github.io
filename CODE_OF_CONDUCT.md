# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

This project aims to maintain a respectful and welcoming environment.

Contributors and visitors are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Use constructive and inclusive language.
- Respect different opinions and perspectives.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and inappropriate behavior.
- Avoid sharing other people's private information without permission.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior related to this project, please contact me through the contact information available on my portfolio.

Reports will be handled respectfully and, where appropriate, confidentially.
